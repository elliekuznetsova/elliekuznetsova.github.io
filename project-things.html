When Do Neural Networks Think Like Humans? Comparing Shallow and Deep CNN Representations on Image Similarity Task

Mar – Jun 2025

With Natalie Abel and Shambhavi Mittal. We compared layer-wise representations from AlexNet and ResNet-50 with human object similarity judgments from the THINGS dataset, using odd-one-out accuracy on 50,000 triplets and RSA. Alignment with human judgments increased in later layers of both models, and AlexNet unexpectedly aligned more closely than ResNet-50.
