Learning, Meaning, and Natural Language Lab, UC San Diego · Sep 2025 – present

Higher- and lower-order concerns in instructor comments: A corpus-based comparison of screencast feedback and written feedback

Northern Arizona University · 2022 – 2026

I led this study of whether feedback type (screencast vs. written) favors content-related or grammar-related comments. I built and curated the Writing Feedback Corpus (5,397 instructor comments on 169 undergraduate papers), managed annotation and coding in Google Sheets, and ran a random forests analysis in R. Instructor variability outweighed feedback modality. Manuscript submitted to Applied Corpus Linguistics.

The role of feedback modality in student–instructor rapport: A study of screencast and written approaches

Northern Arizona University · 2022 – 2026

This study asked whether screencast feedback builds more rapport between instructors and students than written feedback, using independent samples t-tests on the same corpus. I contributed to corpus construction, annotated and coded comments, and wrote the methods and discussion sections. The results challenged assumptions about the benefits of screencast feedback. Manuscript submitted to CALICO Journal.
